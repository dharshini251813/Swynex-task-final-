🚀 Task 4 – Final App Project: PocketSpend Expense Tracker

I'm excited to share my final app project, PocketSpend! 💸

PocketSpend is an Android expense tracker designed to make it easier to record income and expenses, organize transactions by category, and view a simple balance summary.

✨ Key features:
• Add income and expense transactions
• Organize entries by category
• View balance, income, and expenses
• Filter transaction history
• Save data locally on the device

Building this project helped me practice Kotlin, Android UI design, input validation, and local data storage.
